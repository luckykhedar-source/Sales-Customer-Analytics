## Business Recommendations

1. Focus marketing and sales efforts on the highest-performing customer segments.
2. Identify successful strategies used in the top-performing states and apply them to lower-performing regions.
3. Promote high-profit product categories and investigate low-profit categories.
4. Reward and study the performance strategies of top sales employees.
5. Monitor negative month-over-month growth and investigate possible causes such as discounts, lower order volume, or changes in product demand.
